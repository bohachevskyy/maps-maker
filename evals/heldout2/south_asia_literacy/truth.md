- URL/API: https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/literacy-rate-adults.csv (Our World in Data Grapher CSV, 'Literacy rate among adults'; UNESCO Institute for Statistics / World Bank, completed from national sources); latest non-missing year per country; geometry https://naturalearth.s3.amazonaws.com/10m_cultural/ne_10m_admin_0_countries.zip.
- Vintage: OWID file downloaded 2026-10-27; the latest literacy year differs by country: IND 2024, LKA 2024, IRN 2023, BGD 2022, BTN 2022, AFG 2021, PAK 2021, MDV 2019, NPL 2019. Literacy is surveyed or census-based, so years are irregular, not annual.
- Licence: OWID data CC BY 4.0 (UNESCO UIS; World Bank). Boundaries: Natural Earth 1:10m (public domain), Crimea drawn as Ukraine (context only).
- Rows: 9 — all 9 members of the UN M49 sub-region 'Southern Asia' (AFG BGD BTN IND IRN MDV NPL PAK LKA), chosen by an explicit ISO3 list. Columns: key (ISO3), name, value (% of adults 15+ literate), unit, year.
- Caveat: value years are not comparable like for like (Afghanistan 2021 vs India 2024); India's J&K, Pakistan's and China's claims are drawn as Natural Earth's de facto lines. Iran is in 'Southern Asia' in the UN geoscheme, though often thought of as Middle East.
