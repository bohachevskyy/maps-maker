- URL/API: https://www.bls.gov/lau/laucnty24.xlsx (LAUS 'Labor Force Data by County, 2024 Annual Averages', rows with State FIPS 48); also https://www.bls.gov/lau/laucnty25.xlsx for the `rate_2025_11month` column. BLS answers 403 'Access Denied' to non-browser clients; the builder sends browser headers and refuses non-xlsx bytes.
- Vintage: 2024 annual averages as republished May 19, 2026 (after the annual benchmark revision). 2024 is the latest *full* year: the 2025 file is titled 'Labor Force Data by County, 2025 11-Month and Annual Averages*' — October 2025 was never collected (federal shutdown), so its 'annual' figures are 11-month averages.
- Licence: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, public domain. Boundaries: Census cartographic boundary file 2023, 1:500k (public domain).
- Rows: 254 Texas counties, key = 5-digit FIPS; `value` = published unemployment rate (%), not seasonally adjusted, one decimal; `labor_force` and `unemployed` as published.
- Caveat: rates are published to one decimal and are model-based for small counties — the smallest labour force, Kenedy County's, is 97 people — so neighbouring years can differ by whole points. `rate_2025_11month` (2025 average excluding October) is context only; it is not `value`.
