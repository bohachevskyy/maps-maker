- URL/API: https://dataverse.harvard.edu/api/access/datafile/13887042 (`1976-2024-president.csv` in https://dataverse.harvard.edu/dataset.xhtml?persistentId=doi:10.7910/DVN/42MVDX; file list from https://dataverse.harvard.edu/api/datasets/:persistentId/?persistentId=doi:10.7910/DVN/42MVDX); rows `year == 2024`.
- Vintage: MIT Election Data + Science Lab, 'U.S. President 1976–2024', Harvard Dataverse V10 released 2026-09-04; 2024 rows are version 2025-11-20 (certified results).
- Licence: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication). Boundaries: Census cartographic boundary file 2024, 1:20M (public domain).
- Rows: 51 — 50 states plus DC, key `US-XX`. `value` = Trump (R) % − Harris (D) % of valid presidential votes, in percentage points; positive = Republican lead. Each candidate has exactly one row per state in this release (fusion lines such as NY Working Families are pre-summed by MIT).
- Caveat: MIT's `totalvotes` includes undervotes, overvotes, void and spoiled ballots in 8 states (HI, ID, MA, ME, NH, NY, VT, WY); the denominator here drops those rows and keeps third parties, write-ins and Nevada's 'None of these candidates'. Margin on MIT's `totalvotes` is kept as `margin_pp_mit_total`; the two differ by at most 0.49 pp.
