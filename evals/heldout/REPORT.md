## FAILs and proposed spec corrections (specs.toml is untouched)

- **`pacific_typhoons_2024` `line_near=110.8,19.6` FAILs by 10.7 km.** The nearest IBTrACS track
  point to the spec's point is 30.7 km away; Yagi's Hainan landfall fix (6 Sep 2024 09:00 UTC,
  first fix over land, 95 kt JMA) is at 110.8 E, 19.9 N. Proposed: `line_near=110.8,19.9`
  (0 km), or keep the point and state a 35 km tolerance.

## Spec observations that pass but are worth knowing

- **`italy_gdp_regions` `excludes` Ljubljana (14.51, 46.05) is unsatisfiable.** It lies inside
  Italy's own lon/lat extent (Friuli reaches 13.9 E, Bolzano 47.1 N, Puglia 18.5 E), so no
  rectangular frame showing all 21 regions can exclude it; the reference frame contains it
  (Geneva, 6.14 E, is excluded). Proposed: replace Ljubljana with Munich (11.58, 48.14) or Vienna
  (16.37, 48.21), both north of the Italian extent.
- **`zakarpattia_settlements` `count=609±12%`** passes at 604 (-0.8%). KATOTTH (order No. 1321 of
  7 July 2026) lists 604 settlements: 11 cities, 20 селища, 573 villages; 603 are located on OSM.
  Proposed `count=604±3%` if the tolerance should be informative.
- **`japan_quakes_2024`** "in and around Japan" has no boundary: the reference uses the box
  24-46 N, 125-150 E (96 events; the wider 24-47 N, 122-150 E box gives 98 and adds a Taiwan and a
  Primorye event). `count=110±40%` passes (66-154) but 110 is not near either number; 96 is the
  central value. A service-side count for a differently drawn region is legitimate anywhere in
  the window.
- **`london_underground`** `units = [9, 16]` and `count=11±25%` match the 11 sub-surface and deep
  lines (OSM carries a `colour` tag on all of them). The Elizabeth line, DLR, Overground and
  Tram are separate OSM networks and are not Underground. Oxford (-1.26, 51.75) is outside the
  frame; "central London framed" is met by a zone 1-2 inset on a map of the whole network (a
  frame that cut the network at zone 3 would lose Amersham, Epping and Heathrow).
- **`pacific_typhoons_2024` `count=26±30%`** passes at 26 named storms (IBTrACS WP, JMA names;
  Pabuk formed 23 Dec and is counted). IBTrACS also lists the Central Pacific storm Hone in the
  WP file (7 fixes crossing 180 E) and two unnamed depressions; they are excluded.
- **`europe_nuclear_plants`**: OSM is the primary source here, not an audited register. 59 sites,
  152 GW; France 63 GW matches RTE (63 GW). Zaporizhzhia is tagged operating (5,700 MW) although
  all six units are in cold shutdown under Russian occupation; it is kept, drawn grey and
  flagged. Chornobyl (capacity 0) and Lemoiz (disused) are not plants in operation and are
  excluded; Akkuyu (under construction), Beloyarsk (Urals) and Metsamor (Armenia) are outside
  the chosen Europe. Spec `units = [50, 120]` is met (59).
- **`asia_coal_electricity`**: "Asian countries" is not a closed list. The reference uses the UN
  M49 Asia group (plus Taiwan): 51 countries and territories, all with an OWID value. Russia
  (UN: Eastern Europe) is context. `units = [40, 52]` is met (51); adding Russia would make 52.
- **`us_median_age`**: ACS 2020-2024 5-year (released January 2026), the newest on the Census
  site. State margins of error are 0.1-0.4 years, so ranks within one class are not distinct.
- **`poland_voivodeship_population`**: GUS BDL reports 2025-12-31 for all 16 voivodeships (37.3
  million). Natural Earth carries the old alphabetic ISO codes (PL-MZ); the spec's numeric codes
  (PL-14 Mazowieckie, PL-08 Lubuskie) are the current ISO 3166-2:PL and are what truth.csv uses.
- **`south_america_life_expectancy`**: OWID reports French Guiana and the Falkland Islands as
  separate entities, so the truth has 14 rows (spec units 12-15). The 12 sovereign states alone
  satisfy all three spots.
