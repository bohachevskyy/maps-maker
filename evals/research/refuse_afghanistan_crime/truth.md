# refuse_afghanistan_crime (tune)

Prompt: Current crime rate by district in Afghanistan

Expected: **refusal**.

No official or open crime statistics at district level; conflict-event datasets (ACLED, UCDP) are different measures and ACLED needs registration. Proposing one as "crime rate" would misrepresent it.


Caveats:
- An honest alternative (conflict events, UCDP GED) is a different request; the agent may offer it as a suggestion but the proposal for the asked measure must be a refusal.
